Effective Date: September 10, 2026

Gubbins App Development © is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application (“App”). By using the App, you agree to the terms outlined here.

1. Information We Collect

Martian Eye does not collect any personally identifiable information directly from users. The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information in order to use its features.

The App may connect to external services to retrieve publicly available information, including images and data relating to NASA's Mars rover missions. These requests may include technical information such as your IP address, which may be processed by the relevant service in accordance with its own privacy policy.

2. Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or process personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or account details. The App has no account system and requires no registration.

3. Usage Data

Martian Eye does not independently collect or store information about how you use the App.

The App does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to track your activity or create a profile about you.

The App does not collect:

  • Contact information

To provide this functionality, the App may communicate with third-party services and APIs. These services may receive technical information associated with network requests, such as an IP address, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Martian Eye does not control or have access to information that may be collected independently by these third-party services.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties because Martian Eye does not collect or maintain personal information about its users.

6. Data Security

We do not collect or store personal information, and therefore do not maintain a database containing users' personal information.
